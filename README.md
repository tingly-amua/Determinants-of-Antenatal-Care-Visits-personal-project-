## Introduction
This report aims to assess the determinants of antenatal care visits using the Poisson and negative binomial regression models. 
The dependent variable will be antenatal care (ANC) visits which is a count variable. Whereas WHO recommends a minimum of 8 visits, Kenya continues to lag behind with an average of just 4 visits. 
A statistical analysis of these determinants will highlight which are most impactful thus laying a foundation for accurate and evidence-based recoommendations for policy formulation and improvement of the country's health and specifically maternal sector.
